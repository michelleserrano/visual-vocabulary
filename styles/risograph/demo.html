A design language that worships the limitations of a stencil duplicator — two colors of ink, layered on warm paper, slightly misaligned on purpose. The mistake becomes the signature. The third color is the one you didn't print: it emerges where pink and teal overlap.

§ 05 / 07

Cards

05
▤

Three card archetypes from the print shop: ZINE PAGE with registration marks at each corner, PHOTO PRINT with duotone halftone imagery, and STICKER rotated 2° with adhesive-tape corners. All three use multiplied offset blocks instead of drop shadows — because Risograph can't print true black shadows.
