Color Palette

01

The Bauhaus palette has three colors: red (#e8261d), blue (#1a4a8a), yellow (#f7c600) — and black and white. Nothing else. Every color justifies its form. Kandinsky mapped them to shapes: blue→circle, yellow→triangle, red→square.

03

Buttons

03

Buttons map to primary colors. Red = primary action. Blue = secondary. Yellow = tertiary. Each color carries structural meaning, not decoration. On hover: box-shadow: 4px 4px 0 var(--black) — the offset shadow gives Bauhaus pieces their physical dimensionality. On press: shadow collapses, button translates 4px right-down.

05

Cards

05

Cards are rectangles with color-coded tops. Red marks the primary. Blue and yellow mark secondary tiers. Card thumbnails use solid color fields with a geometric shape overlay — circle, triangle, or square. On hover: box-shadow: 5px 5px 0 var(--black) + transform: translate(-2px, -2px). The card gains physical weight.
