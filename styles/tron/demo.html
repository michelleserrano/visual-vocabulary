Buttons

03
◻

TRON buttons are outlines of light. They contain no fill — only boundary. Hover intensifies the glow. Press adds a faint inner fill — the button charges. The primary button is a border, a text-shadow, and a box-shadow. Nothing else.

Cards

05
▣

Cards are data panels in the grid. The cyan top border marks them as active surfaces. Shadow is replaced by glow. On hover, the border brightens and the glow intensifies — the panel becomes more present, more alive.

Design Systems

One Color, All States

The entire visual language derives from a single wavelength at varying opacity. Darkness is not absence of color — it is color at near-zero density.

Glow as Architecture

Box-shadow with three layers creates depth: outer ambient, mid-field glow, and a tight inner flash. Three values — infinite perceived distance.

Contrast on Dark Ground

Cyan on near-black exceeds WCAG AA at full opacity. Maintain minimum 0.55 opacity for all interactive text. Never render critical labels at --t3.
