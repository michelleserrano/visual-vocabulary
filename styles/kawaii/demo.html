Cuteness is a design philosophy. In Japanese cute culture, every interface is an act of care — soft colors say "I won't overwhelm you", round corners say "nothing will hurt here", and sparkles say "I'm happy to be with you."

✿ Form Inputs

✦

🌟 Cards

05
♡

White cards with pastel headers and sparkle decorations. Each card is a gift. Hover: translateY(−4px) + scale(1.01) — the card floats toward you, happy to help. The ✦ in the corner twinkles at its own rhythm. Card headers use gradient pastel fills, never flat or dark.

✦ Navigation

07
🌸

Sakura pill active indicator — the selected tab gets a sakura background and white label inside a pill shape. Inactive tabs: plum-muted text, no fill. Transition uses the spring curve: cubic-bezier(0.34,1.56,0.64,1) — it slightly overshoots, then settles. The navigation is your friend.
