Six cell types. Mix freely.

The bento grammar: STAT, QUOTE, FILL, FEATURE, CHART, ICON. Every well-composed bento page draws from this vocabulary, varying span and tone.

05
▣

Variety is the rule. A bento with all stat cells is boring. A bento with all fills is loud. The 6 cell types — stat / quote / fill / feature / chart / icon — exist to keep compositions varied.
