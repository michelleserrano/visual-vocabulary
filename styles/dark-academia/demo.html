De Coloribus Pulchritudinem · I

The Dark Academia Palette

I

Dark oak, tarnished gold, and parchment. The palette is material — the colour of old wood, old gold, and old paper. No cold greys. No pure blacks. Every shadow has a warm undertone. As Cicero noted, all beauty begins with honest material.

Per Vitruvius, colour should arise from the nature of the material itself — never imposed from without. The Dark Academia palette obeys this ancient principle.
