05

Cards

05
▣

Three card types: Elevated (tonal surface + shadow — interactive, rises on hover), Filled (surface-variant, no shadow — static grouping), Outlined (1px border-outline-var on white surface — lowest emphasis, container role). Each expresses a different level of hierarchy without using color.

Elevated

🎨

Design

Tonal elevation

In MD3, elevation is expressed through a tonal surface (primary color at low opacity) combined with a subtle box-shadow — not shadow alone.

Filled

🌸

Pattern

Surface variant

Filled cards use surface-variant as their background with no shadow. Ideal for static, scannable content like list items or info groupings.

Outlined

🔮

Structure

Outline only

Outlined cards use a 1px outline-variant border on white surface. Use for containers, forms, or cards that should recede in the visual hierarchy.

Controls · Sliders

06
◎

MD3 sliders have the active track in --md-primary and the inactive track in --md-surface-var. The handle is 20px and primary-colored. Track height is just 4px. The two-tone track communicates exact value position without needing numeric labels at every point.

Navigation Bar

07
◻

Material Navigation Bar uses indicator pills, not underlines. The selected destination has a 64×32px pill behind its icon in --md-secondary-container. Icon and label both shift to --md-on-secondary-container. No borders, no underlines. The pill IS the selection signal. Click each destination to see it live.
