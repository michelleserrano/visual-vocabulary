Colors · the gold register

[palette]

Typography · Inter + JetBrains Mono

[type-scale]

Inter for body and display — a warm modern sans with excellent legibility at 15–17px body. JetBrains Mono for everything tagged "metadata": citations, code, timestamps, status pills. Tracking is obsessive at display sizes (-0.022em to -0.04em). Body line-height never below 1.6.

Buttons · pills, chips, shortcuts

[action]

Primary button is solid --accent. Secondary is a 1px --border-2 outline. Ghost is invisible until hover. Action chips are pill-shaped, monospaced, and small — they live under a message, not next to it. Click any primary or chip to see the gold halo flash.

§ 04 / 08

Forms · the chat composer is the marquee

[input]

The chat composer is the single most important component in an AI-native interface. It anchors the screen, accepts paste/drag/voice, shows a character count, and ends with a circular gold send button. All other inputs follow its language: 1px --border-2 at rest, gold glow-ring on focus.

§ 05 / 08

Cards · source, thinking, tool-use

[card]

Three card types carry the AI-native register: Source cards show provenance, Thinking cards show reasoning, and Tool-Use cards show function calls as code. Each has its own anatomy — don't blend them.

The conversation panel renders here. Active sidebar item carries the gold left-rule and a warmer background — never a heavy fill or border, just a tonal shift.
