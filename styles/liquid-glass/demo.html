01 · Palette

Color & Material Tokens

01
◉

Liquid Glass uses rgba(255,255,255,0.18–0.42) fills layered with backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%) brightness(1.05). The saturation boost intensifies the refracted color from beneath — the brightness adds the wet sheen. Without all three, the material reads as Glassmorphism, not Liquid Glass.
