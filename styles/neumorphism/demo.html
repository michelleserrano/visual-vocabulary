Neumorphism

A design language where surfaces appear physically extruded from the background. Depth exists entirely through shadow — no borders, no fills, no gradients. One material, infinite states.

04

Form Inputs

04
⊙

All inputs use --sh-in-sm at rest (recessed into surface) and --sh-in + 0 0 0 2px var(--ac) on focus. Inputs are always inset — they are "holes" you type into, not raised elements.
