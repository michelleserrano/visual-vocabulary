Claymorphism

A design language where every surface becomes a colorful, inflated clay object. Depth lives in a triple shadow stack — colored drop shadow, inner top highlight, inner bottom shadow. No borders. Only volume.

Color Palette

01

Pink clay, sky-blue clay, lime clay, banana clay, lavender clay. The claymorphism palette is Play-Doh: every color is soft, matte, and joyful. No neons. No transparency. No dark surfaces. Each color gets its own tinted drop shadow — the shadow hue matches the object.

Buttons

03
↓

Clay buttons are the core metaphor. Press into the button and it yields — the shadow shrinks as the object depresses. Release and it springs back with cubic-bezier(0.34,1.56,0.64,1) overshoot. Each color gets a matched colored drop shadow: pink button casts a pink shadow.

Cards

05
▣

Cards are clay slabs raised above the surface. The colored top + triple shadow creates the clay volume. The header color is solid matte clay — not a gradient, not a photo. Hover: the card floats 7px higher and the shadow deepens with a spring bounce. No border anywhere.
