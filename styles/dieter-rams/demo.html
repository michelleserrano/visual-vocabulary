Color Palette

01

The palette has three roles: warm off-white (surface), near-black (text), and matte gold (the singular accent). Everything is justified by function, nothing by decoration. Surfaces differ from the background by only 3–6% brightness — depth is tonality, not contrast.

Typography Scale

02

Helvetica Neue. No other choice. Weight 400 for body, 500 for emphasis — never bold. Scale creates hierarchy. Tracking is always slightly open (letter-spacing: 0.01em) on body copy. The type system must do all the work that decoration is forbidden to do.

Buttons

03

The primary button is near-black on off-white — the maximum contrast available in this palette. Gold is used only for the key secondary CTA, once per screen. Never decorate a button. No pill shapes. Border-radius is 2px — Braun uses functional geometry.

04

Form Inputs

04

Inputs are barely differentiated from the background — only a hairline border. Focus reveals them: a gold ring quietly announces attention. Labels are not uppercase. Checkboxes are square (2px radius), dark-filled when checked. Toggles: the only use of gold in this component is the active track.

05

Cards

05

Cards differ from the background by 3% brightness. The gold strip appears once — on the primary card only. Everything else earns its differentiation through tone, not color. Card thumbnails are solid color blocks, never gradients — gradients are not honest.

Principle

Form Follows Function

Every visual element has a purpose. When the function changes, the form must follow. Ornament that does not serve function is waste.

10 principles · 1976

Method

Less, But Better

Reduce to the essential. Do not confuse simplicity with austerity — simplicity is the result of rigorous editing, not neglect.

Weniger, aber besser

Material

Honest to Materials

Do not make materials appear to be what they are not. A plastic surface should not pretend to be metal. Honesty is a material property.

Braun · 1955–1995

Controls · Player

06

The music player channels the Braun T3 radio: labeled controls, a precise slider, restrained typography. Every element can justify its existence. Slider track: gold fill, no radius — Braun sliders are linear instruments. Thumb: white circle, hairline border, gold focus ring.

Navigation Bar

07

Navigation labels are the only navigation. No icons. Helvetica in a row. Active: gold text, gold 2px underline at bottom edge. The minimum effective signal — a single color change and a 2px line carry the full weight of selection state.
