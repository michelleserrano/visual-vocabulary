The New Standard in Dark Interface Design

Dark Editorial

The editorial tradition spans two centuries of considered typography, deliberate negative space, and ink on paper so fine it holds a secret. This system carries that intelligence into dark mode — not as a night-mode afterthought, but as a primary aesthetic. True black. Warm off-white. Gold as punctuation, not decoration.
