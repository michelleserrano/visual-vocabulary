Buttons

03

Buttons simulate real buttons. The 4-stop linear gradient creates a convex surface. The inset shadow on press creates a real depression into the material. Each variant (blue, chrome, red, green) maps to a specific action semantic — not an arbitrary brand color.

04

Form Inputs

04

Inputs are recessed into the surface — physically sunken. The inset shadow (inset 0 2px 6px rgba(0,0,0,0.35)) creates authentic depth. The iOS 6 toggle is the iconic green switch: the thumb slides right, the track turns green, and "ON" fades in on the left side of the track.

05

Cards

05

Cards imitate materials. The leather notebook, the ruled notepad, the aluminum panel. Content lives inside material objects — not abstract containers. The offset box-shadow on the leather card simulates stacked pages — you can see the edges of the paper beneath the cover.

Navigation Bar

07

iOS 6 tab bar: dark gradient faceplate with 50% gloss overlay, pill-shaped selected tab with a blue gradient and inner glow, inactive tabs at 68% white. The top navigation bar is a multi-stop dark gradient with specular highlight — it's meant to look like a physical chrome bar. Table view cells use a subtle gradient to suggest depth.
